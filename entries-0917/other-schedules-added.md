- Console RPC `meta/schedule/list` answers a third member, `other_schedules`, beside `identity` and `schedules`: the schedules in the gateway's schedule store that are not Console schedules and whose target names the agent, oldest first: the ones an agent's schedule tools or the host wrote for it, as a member of the mob (`target: "member"`), as the source of a fork helper (`"fork_helper"`), by an identity (`"identity"`) or by one of its sessions, seated or archived (`"session"`). Each has `schedule_id`, `identity`, `name`, `target`, `content` (the text it sends, or null), `trigger`, `phase`, `revision`, `created_at_utc`, `updated_at_utc` and `next_due_at_utc`, and no sender fields. Not listed: the gateway's own host-runnable schedules, a schedule whose session is created at the fire and is not bound yet, and, when the agent's entry cannot be read, its session schedules. Who reads it: the method's access check is unchanged (`agent.view` on the agent), so a caller who may view an agent now reads there the name, timing and text of the schedules that agents or the host wrote for it, which the Console did not show before. They are listed to read: `meta/schedule/create`, `update`, `pause`, `resume` and `delete` act on Console schedules only and do not accept an id from this list. The member is in every answer and is an empty list where there are none; `schedules` is unchanged. A client that compares the whole answer with a fixed value, or that refuses members it does not know, has to take it; the Console of 0.9.16 and of this release reads `schedules` alone and shows the same pane (no pane shows the new list yet), and neither Meta SDK calls the method. (Read from the source; the new list and the answer for a name the gateway does not know: shown by tests.)

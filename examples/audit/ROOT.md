**Severity:** high. **Verdict:** partial. **Examples:** 029, 030, 031, 032, 033.

Spawn activation therefore falsely satisfies RequiredPeerIngress/AutonomousReadiness/TurnDrivenPeerIngress without attaching a mob-owned runtime inbox drain. The provider response parser and normal turn execution succeeded. The fixture accepts only the exact loopback messages endpoint and serves a complete valid Anthropic SSE sequence in-process; no socket/external provider traffic or real credentials.

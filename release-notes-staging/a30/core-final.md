## Known issue: a member can appear wired but be unable to message its peers

Noted 2026-11-24. On `rkat-rpc` 0.9.14, after adopting a member's identity declaration or changing its tool declaration, the member and its wired peers could no longer message each other even though `mob/members` still showed the edges. On 0.9.14, call `mob/wire` again for each affected pair after each such change, then check delivery at the receiving member. This restored peer messages in both directions in runs on the published binary.

On 0.9.14, avoid adopting a declaration that owns a wiring edge: even a prewired edge could leave the member `repair_blocked`, with later declaration changes committed but not active. An `identity_owned` member whose declaration owned no edge could also become blocked at its next declaration change: in the Core runs this happened after ordinary `mob/wire` to a member whose name sorted after its own, but not to one whose name sorted before it. Then `mob/member_send` can be refused even though `mob/turn_start` runs. In the 0.9.14 runs, a `resolved` reply from `mob/resolve_identity_convergence_block` did not clear this wiring block. See the [0.9.15 wiring fix](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat/blob/v0.9.15/CHANGELOG.md#L2453-L2495) and [remaining custody requirements](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat/blob/v0.9.15/CHANGELOG.md#L4053-L4076). The affected range starts at 0.8.26 by source reading; earlier binaries, REST and the Rust API were not tested here.

## Regression checks

- **Versioned wire semantics, not an evaluator release dependency (60-83, 137-140).** Independently versioned contracts and normalized conformance vectors can preserve Toolkit's separate distribution and host adapters. MobKit still enforces its own protected operations. Neither common vectors nor a supported version allows skipping local policy. Negotiation still refuses unknown obligations. I found no new shadow policy owner or implicit application lockstep.
- **Identity mapping preserves distinct facts (199-265).** Canonical PrincipalRef mapping is compatible with Toolkit requester, executor, credential owner and immediate ingress roles. InputOrigin describes a hop while requester/grant lineage describes authority. Their single admitted binding prevents contradictory combinations without making a legitimate Human -> Peer -> System chain change its original requester. The explicit unavailability of legacy authority prevents an inferred Owner shortcut. The tracer must prove these mappings; naming them does not implement them.
- **Context segmentation does not erase provenance (497-545).** Compatibility precedes hydration/transcript write; model output from mixed input retains the whole dependency domain. Reset creates a fresh session/context generation without inherited content or provider state, while continuing work retains its original mandate and old effects/results stay fenced to their old owner. Thus neither post-inference JSON splitting nor a fresh segment can launder a restricted summary, reset an effect identity or admit a late result by agent name. Incremental notifications require acknowledged coverage and an entry barrier/lease, so they do not become evidence of freshness merely by being silent.
- **Adoption/classification has explicit, bounded authority (547-573).** A classifier's allowed broadening is declared preauthorized release discretion, not proof of semantic classification accuracy or a model-created grant. A legacy bundle becomes an exact new resource under an authority that has rights over it, preserves known restrictions and unknown historical facts, and gains no old-work replay authority. Bundle revocation is meaningful even when source-specific historical revocation cannot be promised. These limits avoid substituting backup possession or ordinary write permission for adoption/release rights.
- **Evidence pending and recovery retain the effect owner (351-376, 628-722).** The proposed orthogonal state belongs to existing owners, parallel attempts have distinct obligations, and uncertain appends reconcile by immutable append identity. Store health does not clear obligations. Owner-store loss fences the old incarnation and requires authoritative non-entry or actual destination reconciliation, not missing-row inference. Reserved alarm evidence is a bounded backend with pre-effect commitment and the same operation ownership; it cannot execute spool rows or create an unaudited fallback. These additions preserve the r2 known-result/unknown/evidence-pending distinctions.
- **The new integration clauses preserve both package roles (224-239, 408-418, 480-495).** Device identity does not imply a known human or physical audience. Credential ceremonies explicitly exclude model/browser observation and login is not effect approval. Shared preparation retains independent waiters. Message classification and application-store/future-reader disclosure prevent treating installation, agent ownership, storage labels, or a channel membership snapshot as sufficient authorization for a frontend reply or monitoring copy.
- **Restore and approval cannot manufacture current permission (185-195, 285-302).** Antirollback requires an authority outside the reverted snapshot and fresh incarnation before protected hydration. Approval is captured through authenticated ingress and the existing approval owner, bound to canonical displayed parameters. Neither copied credentials nor quoted approval text becomes a new grant.

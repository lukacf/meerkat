Meerkat side of #1102 (the OB3 fleet-wide delivery stall): keep the single serialized mob-actor loop as the authority boundary, but stop executing per-member blocking work inside it.

Design verdict (analysis and reviews on #1102): the serialized loop is not the mistake; per-member I/O awaited inline was. `SubmitWork` ran the event-pump ensure (under a mob-wide mutex), the live-session lookup and the autonomous-runtime readiness inline before deferring its reply, and `send_actor_command` had no deadline or cancellation, so one repair-blocked member queued every other member's delivery for the 600 s client budget, and abandoned sends later ran as ghost turns. `ResumeLifecycle` ran two serial per-member readiness loops inline (HomeCore's cold-boot `actor_loop_stalled`).

Changes:
- **Member-local turn work runs off the loop** in per-member single-flight admission lanes (capacity `MEMBER_ADMISSION_LANE_CAPACITY`, typed `MemberAdmissionBacklogFull` when full, checked before the DSL admission), with results re-entering as commands; per-member delivery order is preserved for both ack modes (TurnCompleted deliveries also go through the lane); abandoned callers are skipped instead of executed; the inline-step watchdog names any loop step over 2 s.
- **Typed `MemberReloadRequired` fast rejection** for a durability-degraded member (submit_work, member.send, peer sends), backed by the new `MeerkatMachine::durability_reload_required` / `is_durability_ready` read.
- **Bounded, cancellable deliveries**: `submit_work_with_mode_bounded` and the delivery-identity variant, typed `ActorCommandTimedOut` that guarantees the command did not execute.
- **`ResumeLifecycle` readiness fan-out** as a JoinSet re-entering as commands, ticket-fenced; a member retired mid-fan-out no longer fails the whole Resume; lifecycle commands are re-probed before commit and finish.
- **Bounded event-pump replacement join** off the pump barrier, preserving inherited taps, keep-alive and waiter failure notices.
- **Non-destructive `MobHandle::reload_member_registration`** (same session id and generation), bounded end to end by `MEMBER_RELOAD_TOTAL_TIMEOUT`, refusing typed (`MemberReloadRefused`) while the durable resume authority is unreadable so a still-failing store cannot turn a repairable member into a broken one.

Breaking (CHANGELOG): five new `MobError` variants on a non-`#[non_exhaustive]` enum.

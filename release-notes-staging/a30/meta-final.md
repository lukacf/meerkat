## Known issue: adopting or changing a member can leave its peer wiring silent

Noted 2026-11-24. On the published Meta `rpc_gateway` 0.9.14, adopting a member's identity declaration or changing its tool declaration could stop peer messages in both directions. `reconcile_edges()` could report the edge as already present without restoring delivery; this gateway does not serve `mob/wire`. For this loss of peer messaging, restart the gateway on the same state after each such change and check delivery at the receiving member. The restart restored peer delivery in runs with the default wiring custody.

On 0.9.14, avoid adopting a declaration that owns a wiring edge. A restart is not a remedy for a member blocked by that declaration. If one member's declaration owns an edge to another member under `identity_owned` custody, that other member can remain `repair_blocked`: its owner's change commits without becoming active, and host messages can be refused. The [Core 0.9.14 note](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat/releases/tag/v0.9.14) also describes a declaration with no owned edge that can become blocked after ordinary wiring. The comparison of the two member-name orders was run on Core, not on this gateway. See the [0.9.15 Meta upgrade note](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat/blob/meta-v0.9.15/meta/CHANGELOG.md#L576-L601) and [peer-wiring fix](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat/blob/v0.9.15/CHANGELOG.md#L2453-L2495). The 0.9.14 runs used a gateway without an identity roster; 0.8.26 through 0.9.13 were read from source, not run.

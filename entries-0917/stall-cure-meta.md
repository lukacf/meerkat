- Mob members: a response to a peer request without a result no longer stops the member that sent the request (a gateway whose state is on disk; at least Meta 0.9.9 through 0.9.16: Meerkat's runtime had it from 0.9.5, and Meta releases before 0.9.9 were not read; see the Known limitation printed under 0.9.16). The change is in Meerkat's runtime: see Meerkat's changelog for this release, Fixed, "Peer requests: a response to a peer request without a result no longer stops the requester", for what changes, what it does not do for a member that an earlier release stopped, and the case that stays. (Shown by tests and by runs on an `rkat-rpc` and on a Meta gateway built from this release's source, the gateway driven by the published 0.9.16 Python SDK: after a progress response `accepted` followed by the final response with its result, three of three, and after a final response `completed` without a result, three of three, the member that sent the request ran its later message and the log had neither line of the stop.)

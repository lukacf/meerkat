## A12-001: Implementer prompt requires a reviewer-driven revision loop absent from the implement pack

**Candidate severity:** medium. **Independent verdict:** confirm.

The injected prompt says tasks come from the reviewer, instructs direct delivery to that reviewer, and requires revision until approval. In fact the host dispatches implementation first and forwards its returned text to one later reviewer turn. Waiting for approval or treating a peer send as flow completion can stall or confuse the first step.

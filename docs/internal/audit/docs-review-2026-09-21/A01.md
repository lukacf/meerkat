## A01-002: The release recipe omits the mandatory exact-tree main-push semver readiness artifact

**Candidate severity:** high. **Independent verdict:** confirm.

The step-by-step guide says to wait for exact-main CI and then tag, and the root instructions present a combined commit/tag push without warning about a separate prerequisite. A tag created before Release semver readiness has succeeded on the version-bumped exact tree, or after its evidence expires, fails the semver gate even when local preflight and ordinary CI are green. This is a concrete missing release dependency, not merely a missing explanatory example. Asset-only/Web-only exemptions and explicit completed-measurement recovery are distinct paths and must not be conflated with the normal tag path.

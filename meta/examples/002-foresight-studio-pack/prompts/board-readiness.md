# Board-Readiness Prompt

Run a board-readiness pass for the Borealis field-service copilot launch.

First scan the signal lake, then ask at least two relevant peers for a challenge
or evidence card, score the launch thesis, and finish with a concise board memo
recommendation. Call out:

- the strongest evidence
- the largest unresolved risk
- the next validation sprint
- whether Q3 should be lighthouse-only or broad launch

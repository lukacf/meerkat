Mob announcements carry developments to the members that asked for them. The
host, or a member through its tools, announces a development to a mob. Every
member subscription that names that mob and one of the announcement's topics
captures it, and each subscriber receives what it is owed in bounded batches.
Each batch is one Meerkat host continuation keyed by its batch id.

Mob announcements are optional, and a persistent, identity-first host
enables them the same way on every surface: `rpc_gateway` with
`runtime_options.mob_announcements = {"enabled": true}`, `meta_gateway` with
its `mob_announcements` init param, and a Rust host with
`UnifiedRuntimeBuilder::mob_announcements`. Each refuses a configuration it
cannot compose before it builds anything. The batch, retention and limit
options are in [Configuration](/mobkit/reference/configuration). Without them,
nothing opens the announcement database (`mob_announcements.sqlite3`), and
the methods answer `mob_announcements_unavailable`.

The console serves the same methods on `/console/rpc`. Every one of them,
the reads included, needs the `runtime.admin` grant, and a read-only console
refuses the ones that change state.

## Announcements

An announcement belongs to one mob hosted on the gateway, and its source key
identifies it within that mob. Announcing the same key with the same content
again replays the first announcement; the same key with other content is
refused as a conflict. An announcement names its topics and its body, and can
carry a correlation id and the announcements that caused it. Batch bodies
quote each announcement and name its mob and its announcer.

## Subscriptions

A subscription belongs to one member, `{mob_id, identity}`, and names the
topics it wants from the mobs it names (the gateway's own mob by default). It
captures announcements from now on, or from a frontier of per-mob sequence
numbers. Pause, resume and cancel name the revision they expect, so a stale
caller is refused instead of overwriting a newer change.

A subscriber can be:

- a registered identity of the gateway's mob that the gateway's runtime serves;
- a member of another mob hosted on the gateway, such as a mob a member
  created, subscribed by the host by its plain roster id;
- a member of a mob on a peer gateway, reached through
  [keyed inject](/mobkit/concepts/access-control#keyed-inject) when this gateway's
  contact directory files that mob with a pinned key and this gateway holds a
  signing key.

Members announce and subscribe for themselves through the `mob_announcements`
tool bundle (`mob_announce` and `mob_subscription`) when their profile lists
it in `tools.rust_bundles`. A member of a mob a member created gets them only
under the host's `member_tools_in_child_mobs` ceiling, by naming
`mob_announcements` in its own profile's `tools.bundles`. A member announces
into its own mob as itself and manages only its own subscriptions. It may name
its own mob and the mobs above and below it among the mobs members created,
never a sibling's. Each call is attributed to exactly one member or refused,
within per-member limits.

## Delivery

- An announcement is captured for every matching subscription in the same
  transaction that records it.
- A lost acknowledgment is reconciled from the native status, without a second
  submission. A batch is submitted again only after a signed answer that
  nothing was committed.
- A delivery stranded on a retired incarnation moves to a successor batch that
  names its predecessor.
- What waited past the retention window for a delivery that never started
  expires, and the member's next batch reports it as a gap. A batch whose
  outcome, or whose admission, was never confirmed settles without being
  submitted again, and the next batch reports its announcements as not
  confirmed.
- Reading a batch shows the native status and the admitted input's runtime
  state. Admission is not processing: the input's state says what the
  runtime did with it.

## When a subscription ends

A subscription ends, with a typed reason, only on a final fact:

- every mob it named was destroyed;
- for a member of another mob hosted here, that mob was destroyed, created
  again, completed or reset after the subscription was made;
- a peer gateway answered, signed, that its member departed. No gateway of
  this release records a final fact that a member left, so none gives that
  answer yet.

A member that is not registered, or not served where its deliveries go, ends
nothing: it may be registered or served again. Neither does a member that
left this gateway, is governed, or has no live incarnation. Its deliveries
wait, and the subscription's `marker` says why and on what evidence (a
verified signed answer, an unverified one, or this gateway's own fact). Its
owed batches expire after the retention window, and `subscriptions/cancel`
ends a subscription whose member is not coming back.
